Alev Ersan, A Room in Three Rites, 2017, Sound, voice and drawings

Drawings by Alev Ersan
Collaborative Narrative by Burcu Borhan Türeli, Suna Kafadar and Alev Ersan
Sound design by Cenker Kökten

We are infected with time, busy in our minds and fragile in our inflexible bodies. With projectile necks and extended arms we reach within, into our drowned-out memories to tell the stories we carry molecularly. This room acts as a shared gesture, intended to hand-make the story that we need to hear, that we’ve forgotten, that is our demon, our parent or grandparent.
It is to undo the conditions that have brought us into a chronic state.
Let the animals in.
