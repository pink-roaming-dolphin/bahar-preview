A Corner in the World, Walk Around the Corner Bomonti, 2017, Performance walk

Conceptualized by A Corner in the World, Walk Around the Corner is a performance walk that brings together short works by performing artists and companies based in Turkey. In various corners of a selected neighbourhood in the city, the performance walk invites audiences to experience a collective portrait of ‘what is around the corner’ in the performance scene in Turkey.


The first edition of Walk Around the Corner took place as part of A Corner in the World Performing Arts Festival in October 2016 in Kadıköy. This edition will take place in Bomonti.


The walk will start at the entrance of Bomontiada.

SUNDAY MAY 14, 16:00 Bomontiada Avlu
