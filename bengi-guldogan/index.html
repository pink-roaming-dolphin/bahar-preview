Bengi Güldoğan, Kalhana, 2017, Installation

“...rural areas don’t deny unreal things the way cities do.”


Cynan Jones, The Long Dry

In an agricultural geography of thousands of years, a family history, harvest times, agricultural machines, spiral machines, harvesters, batches of crops, pests, remains...


Kalhana is an installation that comprises a couple of pieces that are about what is happening in the rural, true stories and dreamt moments.
