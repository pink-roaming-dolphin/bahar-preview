Bahar is a commissioning platform for producing works across disciplines and cultural productions.

BAHAR: What is it?

Bahar brings together a group of participants to discursively produce an acute commentary around a particular timely issue. Furthermore, it creates multivalent critical platforms in order to invite speakers, curate film programs and commission new research projects (planting seeds/research phase), which cultivate grounds for the production of artworks, performances and publications (harvesting/commissions phase).
